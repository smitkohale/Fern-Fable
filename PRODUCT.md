# PRODUCT.md: Fern & Fable

Made-up brand for a portfolio Shopify theme. Everything here is fictional and only for display.

## The brand
- **Name:** Fern & Fable
- **One line:** Cotton linens printed by hand with carved wooden blocks, for Indian tables and homes.
- **Sells:** home textiles in 5 collections: Kitchen, Table, Bed, Living, Gifts. 26 products, ₹599 to ₹3,299 (see `products.csv`).
- **Story:** every piece passes through three pairs of hands: the carver cuts the block, the dyer mixes indigo, madder and turmeric, the printer stamps the cloth one block at a time. Each piece carries that story, its fable. Showing the craft (blocks, dye, hands, fabric) is the brand's big visual idea.

## Audience
- 25 to 40, living in Indian cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Pune).
- Setting up first homes, love having friends over, buy thoughtful gifts.
- Find brands on Instagram, shop mostly on their phones.
- They already know "block print". What they want to know: will it look good on my table, is it worth the price, will it arrive in time for the dinner or the festival.

## What the site must do
- **Primary action:** add a product to the cart (on the homepage: get people into a product or collection fast).
- **Secondary:** gifting (gift finder, gift wrap, Festive Edit) and the newsletter.
- **Top objections:** colour runs in the wash, the photos look better than the real thing, delivery before the occasion, price compared to mass market linens. The site answers these with care notes, close up print photos, a shipping promise and the craft story.

## Personality
- **Is:** bold, warm, handmade, generous.
- **Is not:** beige and minimal, kitschy, a souvenir shop, a generic "ethnic" marketplace.
- **Voice:** plain and warm, like a friend who knows fabric. Short sentences. Specific details (sizes, wash care, how it was printed). No filler words (see the banned list in the landing-page-design skill). Sentence case for headings and buttons. No hyphens in theme copy.

## Signature moments (go big here only)
1. **Hero:** "Set a table they'll remember." A printed runner unfurls onto a table in 3D (three.js), the headline animates in. Still photo or video fallback.
2. **Product reels:** a row of short, muted, vertical videos, each linked to a product with quick add.
3. **Shop the look, "The Indigo Table":** dots on a styled table photo open product cards, plus "Add the whole look".
4. **The craft story:** the section stays on screen while three steps scroll past (carve, dye, print), with a 3D carved block that ends by stamping the cloth.
5. **Tagline reveal:** a large two line statement whose words ink in one by one on scroll.

Everything else (collection, product, cart, search) stays calm, fast and clear.

## Seasonal layer
Launching near Christmas: a hint of festive, never a takeover. Festive Edit in the announcement bar and gift finder, Winter Berry print in the hero cloth, a few slow drifting block printed stars. All behind one `festive_mode` theme setting.

## Demo labels
- Footer line: "Demo store: a portfolio project. Fern & Fable is not a real brand."
- No personal name anywhere on the site.
- Reviews and testimonials are labelled as sample reviews.
